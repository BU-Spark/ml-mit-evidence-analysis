# Research Summaries

A collection of summaries from various research papers and articles related to technology and its applications in combating human trafficking and image recognition.

## 1. Multimedia Metadata-based Forensics in Human Trafficking Web Data

- **Name**: Qinghui Zhu
- **Title**: Multimedia Metadata-based Forensics in Human Trafficking Web Data
- **Link**: [https://e.humanities.uva.nl/publications/2016/murd_sexi16.pdf#page=10](https://e.humanities.uva.nl/publications/2016/murd_sexi16.pdf#page=10)
- **Summary**: 
  - The paper presents the system created by the team that utilizing multimedia metadata to enhance the identification and connection of images and videos within the human trafficking domain.Unlike traditional computer vision approaches, Image Space leverages metadata—ranging from physical attributes and geolocation to device details—for a comprehensive analysis. This innovative approach facilitates the linking of multimedia content to relevant ads and helps uncover connections between victims and perpetrators. The system represents a significant advancement in combining ad-based data with metadata-based forensics, offering new possibilities for research and investigation in human trafficking. 
- **Note**:
- **Sign**: Qinghui Zhu

## 2. Deformation Models for Image Recognition

- **Name**: Jiawei Sun
- **Title**: Deformation Models for Image Recognition
- **Link**: [https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/stamp/stamp.jsp?tp=&arnumber=4250467](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/stamp/stamp.jsp?tp=&arnumber=4250467)
- **Summary**: 
  - The paper presents nonlinear deformation models for image recognition, effective for images with local changes like handwritten digits and medical images. It demonstrates that a simple yet nonlinear model can achieve competitive performance across different tasks. Notably, it achieves very low error rates on the MNIST dataset and in medical image categorization, highlighting the balance between algorithm complexity and performance, the importance of pixel context, and the utility of true 2D approaches.
- **Note**: Model regarding image recognition.
- **Sign**: Jiawei Sun

## 3. Turning technology against human traffickers

- **Name**: Ilya Bulygin
- **Title**: Turning technology against human traffickers
- **Link**: [https://news.mit.edu/2021/turning-technology-against-human-traffickers-0506](https://news.mit.edu/2021/turning-technology-against-human-traffickers-0506)
- **Summary**: 
  - Describes MIT Lincoln Laboratory's approach to combat human trafficking by developing machine learning algorithms to parse online commercial sex ads for trafficking indicators and creating systems to organize and analyze digital evidence, aiding in investigations and prosecutions.
- **Note**: Relevant to see the main idea of the project and the methods used.
- **Sign**: Ilya Bulygin

## 5. 

- **Name**: Nurassyl Medeu
- **Title**: DINOv2: Learning Robust Visual Features without Supervision
- **Link**: [https://arxiv.org/pdf/2304.07193v2.pdf](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2304.07193v2.pdf)
- **Summary**: 
  - This paper discusses image classification in the context of visual features learned by the DINOv2 model. Specifically, it mentions training a linear classifier on top of the model's frozen features and testing it on datasets like iNaturalist and Places205. The results show that DINOv2 performs better than competing models.
- **Note**: This paper generally talks about the approach to pretraining computer vision models using self-supervised learning methods, which can be useful for our project.
- **Sign**: Nurassyl Medeu
